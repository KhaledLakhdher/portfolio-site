Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to apply for the Data Analyst / Data Specialist position in Nyon. During my internship at CTT, I built an end-to-end pipeline to analyze and classify thousands of customer reviews. I handled the full data lifecycle --- preprocessing, cleaning, and quality checks, then structuring and modeling --- and automated the export of the results into clear Excel reports that non-technical stakeholders could act on directly. That experience taught me how to turn messy, real-world data into validated, documented, and decision-ready outputs, which is precisely the structuring, quality, and result-popularization work this role calls for.

I approach data with a rigorous, quality-first mindset. I am comfortable with Python (Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib) and SQL, and I have designed platforms for storing, organizing, and serving structured and vector data, so I am at ease contributing to the tools that support data management.

I work fluently in English (TOEIC 825/990) and French (DELF B2), which lets me collaborate comfortably in a French-speaking, multidisciplinary environment. I am organized and used to tracking several projects in parallel with a rigorous approach, and I am genuinely eager to grow into an autonomous reference on data questions within your team. The opportunity to apply advanced data exploitation to high-level medical research is exactly the kind of meaningful, evolving challenge I am looking for.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my analytical background and attention to data quality could support Sword Services' research goals. Thank you for your time and consideration.
