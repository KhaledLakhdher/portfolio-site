I am applying for the \Role\ role at Bending Spoons. I build data systems that are correct, reliable, and genuinely useful, and I work AI-first, weaving machine intelligence into how I reason and build. I treat data correctness as non-negotiable. During my internship at CTT, I built an end-to-end pipeline over thousands of customer records: I preprocessed, cleaned, and validated the data, then structured and modeled it, and automated the export of the results into reports that non-technical teams could act on directly. Across my engineering projects I evaluate systems against formal metrics such as nDCG and MRR, and I build validation layers that verify every output before it is trusted. Defining metrics, validating data, and hardening the robustness of reporting is precisely the kind of work I gravitate toward.

I also like building the tooling that other people rely on. I designed \textbf{Nexus AI}, a multi-tenant platform that stores, organizes, and serves structured and vector data (FastAPI, PostgreSQL/pgvector, Redis Streams) with real-time monitoring and per-run cost tracking: internal data tooling built for scale. I move comfortably across diverse stacks (Python, FastAPI, Django, Next.js, PostgreSQL/pgvector, Redis, Docker), and I use AI tools every day to move faster, surface ideas, and study how competing products work, including building my own portfolio's grounded AI assistant, which answers only from verified data.

I try to reason from first principles, hold myself to a rising bar, and get my hands dirty for the team. I am early in my career, but I took your note to heart: that potential and verifiable ability matter more than a perfect CV. I would rather be measured by what I can do than by years on paper, and I would welcome your tests as the chance to show exactly that.

I would be thrilled to contribute to Bending Spoons and to grow at the rate you promise talented people. Thank you for your time and consideration.
